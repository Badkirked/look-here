Look Here settings

Local export works without a server. Uploads are optional and go only to the receiver you configure here.

HTTP exposes screenshots and tokens to anyone able to intercept that network traffic. Use HTTPS whenever possible. The receiver must enforce its own authentication and storage limits.

Local screenshots

Drafts expire after 24 hours and are limited to the latest 10 captures. Cleanup runs when the browser is running. Closing a tab does not immediately delete its draft; the editor’s Close button does.
